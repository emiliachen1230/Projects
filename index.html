About
Me

Designing interfaces, systems, and visual structures with clarity, logic, and sensitivity.

I’m a Product and UI/UX Design Engineer interested in building experiences where research, interaction flow, and visual language work as one system. My practice moves between interface design, product thinking, and design implementation, with a focus on clarity, structure, and precise execution.

Interface & UX
User flow, wireframing, information architecture, and high-fidelity interface design.

Product Thinking
Research synthesis, service logic, scenario building, and concept development.

Visual Systems
Brand extension, layout rhythm, and consistent design language across touchpoints.

Prototype & Front-end
HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and design-to-code translation for interactive prototypes.

I design by aligning logic, interaction, and form into one readable system.
I’m interested in work where design is not decoration, but structure made visible.
