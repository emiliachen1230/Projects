Rondo is an interactive doorbell concept that reframes arrival as a musical threshold experience. Developed as a cross-disciplinary product and hardware prototype, the project translates emotional intent into a clear interaction model by combining crafted form, sensing logic, Arduino programming, and audio feedback. Rather than presenting a speculative object alone, the project demonstrates how a familiar household touchpoint can be redefined through concept framing, behavioral flow, and prototype-ready thinking.

Interactive Doorbell
as Musical Ritual

Rondo reimagines the doorbell as an object that communicates presence through melody, tactile engagement, and crafted form. Instead of limiting arrival to a single alert sound, the device uses key-based interaction, sensing logic, and audio output to create a richer threshold experience. The concept explores how an everyday product can carry emotional value while remaining legible as a functional interactive device.

From Alert Sound
to Emotional Interface

The project uses music not as decoration, but as interaction logic. Melody becomes the medium through which arrival is announced, personality is expressed, and shared space feels less mechanical. This shift turns the product from a notifier into an interface for atmosphere, recognition, and social warmth.

Use Contexts
and Placement Scenarios

Rondo was imagined for places where the threshold matters, including home entrances, studio receptions, boutique hospitality settings, and small cultural venues. In each context, the object functions as more than a bell: it becomes a first gesture of atmosphere, signaling whether a space feels playful, welcoming, intimate, or ceremonial before a conversation even begins.

Home Entrance

Placed at a home entry, Rondo turns the ordinary act of arriving into a softer and more personal ritual. Instead of functioning as a sharp alert, it creates a gentle moment of recognition between the visitor and the resident, making the threshold feel warmer and more intimate.

Studio Reception

In a studio or creative workspace, Rondo works as both a signaling device and an identity object. Its musical response adds personality to the entrance experience, helping the space communicate openness, creativity, and a more memorable first impression.

Boutique Hospitality

For boutique hotels, guesthouses, or curated hospitality spaces, Rondo introduces a welcoming gesture that feels more refined than a conventional buzzer. The interaction supports an atmosphere of care and distinctiveness, turning a practical function into part of the brand experience.

Gallery or Cultural Venue

Within galleries, exhibition spaces, or cultural venues, Rondo can operate as a transitional object between outside noise and inside experience. Its sound-based interaction creates a subtle psychological shift, preparing visitors to enter a more attentive and sensory environment.

Shared Community Space

In shared residential or community-based settings, Rondo encourages a more human and less mechanical form of contact. The musical interface softens the boundary between private and shared space, helping entrances feel less abrupt and more socially inviting.

Concept Retail or Lifestyle Space

For concept stores or lifestyle-oriented commercial spaces, Rondo functions as an atmospheric touchpoint that extends the spatial narrative. Rather than simply announcing arrival, it becomes part of the environment’s tone, reinforcing the store’s aesthetic character and emotional positioning.

Project Overview

Interaction Logic
Five keys act as the main interaction surface. Users can trigger notes directly, while sensing and playback logic transform simple touch into a more performative entry experience. Bluetooth playback extends the object from doorbell to ambient sound device.
Form Language
The silhouette references brass instruments and acoustic resonance, helping the object read as both device and sound sculpture. This visual borrowing gives the project immediate character while supporting the sonic identity of the concept.
Dimensions
H650 × W700 × D200 mm. This scale was chosen to give the object enough wall presence to read as a threshold feature, while keeping the keys and sound tube within a comfortable zone for approach, touch, and visual recognition.
Material Direction
Ebony, Brass, Plastic, PCB
Prototype Skills
Arduino programming, circuit integration, physical prototyping, craft fabrication, 3D construction, and CNC-supported making.

Process Explanation
and Design Flow

FROM THRESHOLD IDEA TO WORKING OBJECT

Rondo was developed as a translation process rather than a single styling exercise. The work began by reframing the doorbell as a threshold ritual, then moved through form studies, interaction logic, and electronics prototyping until the concept could perform as a believable object. This sequence matters because the project is strongest when sound, bodily gesture, and fabrication logic all support the same emotional proposition.

01

Reframing the Doorbell

The starting point was not hardware, but the social moment of arrival. The design question asked how a doorbell could stop behaving like a harsh notifier and begin acting as a welcoming interface with tone, rhythm, and presence.

02

Translating Music into Form

Sketches explored how brass-instrument references, sound tubes, and key layouts could carry musical identity without collapsing into literal imitation. The body had to feel sculptural, playable, and still understandable as an entry device.

03

Building the Interaction Logic

The project then shifted into behavioral design. Touch became the main affordance, note playback became confirmation, and the sound tube plus speaker assembly gave the response a visible and audible sense of consequence.

04

Prototyping Electronics and PCB

Finally, the concept was grounded through Arduino programming, circuit planning, debugging, and a custom PCB workflow. This stage tested whether the form could genuinely host the logic it promised, turning speculation into a functioning prototype direction.

Hardware Logic and Prototype Electronics

Arduino Programming · Custom PCB Development

The electronics were developed as a complete making process from zero to a working custom PCB, rather than assembled from prefabricated modules alone. From defining the control logic and wiring architecture, to prototyping the circuit, soldering components, debugging signal flow, and integrating the board into the physical body, this stage translated the project from form concept into a functioning interactive instrument. It shows that the work extends beyond appearance into full-stack object making at the scale of behavior, electronics, and fabrication.

Interaction Flow

Interaction was designed around immediacy, legibility, and delight. The keys act as the primary affordance, inviting touch without written instruction; contact then triggers note playback, while the speaker and sound-tube assembly deliver auditory and visual confirmation. The result is a concise behavioral model in which approach leads to recognition, touch leads to response, and response shapes the emotional tone of arrival.

Music as Shared Language

One of the project’s central ideas is that music can communicate before language does. By embedding melody into a threshold object, Rondo suggests a more open and less transactional mode of greeting, one that can move across domestic, social, and cultural contexts without requiring explanation first.

Outcome Signal

Project Outcome

Rondo resulted in a concept prototype that merged crafted product form, interactive behavior, and prototype electronics into one coherent experience. The project framed the doorbell not as a minor accessory, but as a designed threshold event.

Skills Demonstrated

Future Development
